# Completed A and deferred current marks

This note discharges an **abstract deferred-mark lemma under explicit premises**.
It does not discharge the native logical-history induction, the shadow/Poisson
comparison, native survivor blindness, or a Lean theorem. The completed-A
comparison in REPORT.tex remains conditional on those separate obligations.

## Reference experiment and premises

Fix a public job shape and a logical trace. Let K_A be its complete distinct
older key set, determined independently of the current random family. Let Z
contain old placement, version decisions, old-family randomness, source ranks,
and public scheduling information. For each x in K_A sample a uniform w-bit
word W_x, independently across distinct keys and independently of Z. Shared
versions of x use the **same** W_x; they are not additional trials.

1. Before A routing, chronological deduplication partitions K_A into retained
   A main and an old A tier using Z only. No current-word selection occurs.
2. At binary depth i a route/cap may inspect Z and bits below i only. Its
   source order, survivor choices, pauses and resumed state are measurable
   with respect to that information. No shed returns to main in the counted
   interval. Capacities and route geometry are public.
3. A preparation runs with the B region blank and never selects A against B.
   The reference route exists for every word assignment, including assignments
   on which actual B production or A storage fails. Unlimited analytical
   storage can implement this continuation; it does not change survivors.
4. Operations on disjoint blocks commute, or an explicit simulation identifies
   their state with the level-ordered reference route. Scheduler choices that
   inspect additional current bits are excluded by premise 2.
5. At the completed boundary, old-tier keys and all A sheds are preserved
   once. Source copies are folded by exact key, without deleting a key merely
   because it has a B copy. Final A/B dedup occurs only after this boundary.

These are premises to prove about the implementation, not consequences of
uniform words or distinct residual keys. Fixed-trace independence also needs
a source-history theorem; a trace chosen by observing current words need not
satisfy it.

## Deferred-mark lemma

Write W_x = L_x + 2^i V_x and let F_i be the sigma field generated by Z and
all L_x, x in K_A. Conditional on F_i, the V_x remain mutually independent,
each uniform on {0,...,2^(w-i)-1}. Any F_i-measurable partition of K_A into
main, old-tier and already-shed keys therefore retains that product suffix
law, jointly across all three classes.

Proof: for a fixed low-word vector l, the number of full-word assignments
with suffix vector v is one for every v. Product uniformity gives probability
2^(-(w-i)|K_A|) to every v conditional on Z and l. A measurable partition is
a function of Z,l, so fixing it changes no suffix probability. This proves
joint independence, including disjoint classes; it does not make those
classes' low bits independent. An induction over caps establishes that the
partition at depth i is measurable, since each transition uses only the
newly exposed bit and the previous measurable state. Repeated copies expose
one key's mark once and must share it throughout the induction.

For d = r 2^e with odd r and b = k+e < w, the terminal mark
floor(W_x/2^b) mod r has law
q_a = #{0 <= h < 2^(w-b): h mod r = a} / 2^(w-b).
It is independent across keys and of the low b bits. It is generally not
exactly uniform over radix classes. The same conditional argument applies
before its first exposure. This is the actual finite-word law, without an
extra fictitious independent radix draw for shed shadows.

The lemma applies at an intermediate frontier, **not** to the identity of
the final completed residual pool after all routing bits have been read.
Later losses depend on later bits. Their dependence is retained by routing
each key's shadow with that same mark and applying the joint (M,R) shadow
comparison; replacing losses at successive stages by independent samples is
not justified by this lemma.

## Early padding without conditioning on success

At depth I collect the old tier and early sheds in their F_I-measurable order.
Given F_I, their coarse-range indicators have independent Bernoulli law p_a
for the relevant remaining binary marks and radix mark. Suppose their count
N is at most the public P on every history before the first A capacity
failure. Define the **reference list on every assignment** by taking the
first min(N,P) keys; append P-min(N,P) fresh independent dummy marks.
Its range count has Bin(P,p_a) law conditional on F_I, hence also
unconditionally, and dominates the actual early count whenever N <= P.
Truncation makes the object defined on earlier-failure assignments too.
This proves the early-padding step without selecting the successful runs.
The dummy count is conditionally independent of the marks of remaining main
keys; the remaining main state itself can depend on F_I. This alone does
not establish independence after replacing that main state by a Poisson
comparison. The imported joint comparison must preserve the needed test.

For later A losses define the complete reference route first, retain every
shed shadow, and apply the existing backward increasing-differences and
ideal-position comparison to their joint count. Retained-main membership can
be arbitrary as a function of Z; its containment in complete old positions
is the required premise. Distinctness cannot substitute for that premise.

Let E_A be the resulting unconditional completed-A range witness and E_B
be the direct producer witness. If the first failure is A storage/range,
all earlier tests succeeded and the pointwise simulation gives containment
in E_A. If B failed earlier, the first failure is in E_B instead. Thus the
relevant contribution is bounded by P(E_A)+P(E_B), using the original
reference measure. No assertion about P(E_A | E_B^c) is required. Shared
A/B keys and same-family direct B selection make such conditioning unsafe.

All A candidates remain available in this comparison until B selection is
applied at finish. Deleting candidates afterward only decreases an A range
count, but that does not make the selected subset an independent family.
The final-union excess uses the fixed distinct logical union and its current
words, with shared keys counted once. It is another unconditional witness.

## Finite regressions and outstanding obligations

`code/test_direct_frontier.py` exhaustively checks joint suffix laws after
an asymmetric low-bit cap, conservation of every shed key across route
stages, and the radix mark law. It also records counterexamples to conditioning
on producer success, distinct-key independence after B selection, survivor
selection using deferred bits, and independent marks for successive losses.
These small models test the mathematical distinction; they do not extract
or validate the native implementation.

Still required: native source/history and current-family independence;
exact-key/version correctness; survivor and metadata measurability; schedule
commutation and pauses; unconditional reference continuation; applicability
of the imported ideal-position/shadow comparison including joint early
padding; and complete first-failure exposure coverage. This note establishes
neither an unconditional hierarchy bound nor a Lean proof. In the ideal
reference model, the Lean packages `IdealDirectA` and `IdealSepA` derive the
corresponding two-family old-home densities directly (see
[LEAN_CLAIMS.md](LEAN_CLAIMS.md#api-semantics-and-the-conditional-native-adapter)).
